# Product overview
**Proposition.** Workers: *Your time. Your skills. Your way to earn.* Businesses: *Find the right people. Get the work done.*

It is a flexible talent and work marketplace — not a job board — combining job discovery, project work, short shifts, local tasks and recurring work. Workers are not limited to any demographic (parents, retirees, students, independents and professionals are all in scope); matching never uses protected characteristics.

**Principles implemented:** availability is a first-class input to matching (real days/hours, not a preference tag); compensation is numeric and its basis (hourly/fixed/shift/recurring) is always stated and validated; recommendations carry a plain-language explanation and an honest confidence figure; verification badges reflect recorded decisions only; no pressure to accept (no auto-accept; applying is optional and withdrawal is always possible).

**Three portals on one platform:** public site, worker portal (`/worker`), business portal (`/business`), admin portal (`/admin`) — one database, one auth system, one permission model.
