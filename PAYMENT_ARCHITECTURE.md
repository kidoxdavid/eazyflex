# Payment architecture — **DESIGN ONLY. Nothing here is implemented.**
No payment code, tables, keys or UI exist; the UI says payments are not active, and no payment outcome is ever simulated. Planned (Phase 3): Stripe Connect (Canada) with hosted onboarding for workers, PaymentIntents created server-side with idempotency keys, webhook endpoint with signature verification and a `payment_webhook_events` dedupe table, a double-entry `financial_ledger`, configurable `pricing_rules` (commission %, minimums, category overrides, promotions — never hard-coded; 15% in the brief is illustrative), states created→pending→authorised→processing→paid→(partially_)refunded|disputed|failed|cancelled driven only by provider webhooks, and Finance-Administrator-only permission for pricing and refunds. No card data is stored. Regulatory review (money transmission, tax reporting, consumer protection) is required before launch.
